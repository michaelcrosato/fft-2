# FINAL FEALTY TACTICS — *The Chronicle of the Twelve Braves*

A low‑poly, modern-rendered tactical RPG that is an unashamed love letter to the
classic isometric war-drama tactics games of the late 90s. Tone: **serious
medieval political tragedy** — class struggle, betrayal, a corrupt church,
demons hiding behind holy relics — delivered in slightly archaic, earnest prose,
with small moments of warmth and wry humour between friends. Never parody.

The title is the one playful thing: *fealty* is what every character owes someone,
and what almost everyone betrays.

## 2. Story outline (battle ids are fixed — content files must use them)

Every battle has `pre` and `post` scenes with full dialogue. Chapters open with a
title card. Tessa, Alys and other non-combatants appear in scenes only.

### Prologue — "The Abbey" (1 battle)
Historian **Alazar Durant** introduces "the tale the Church burned". Five riders
approach **Orvelle Abbey**. Princess Oriane prays; Adria guards her; the mercenary
captain **Garmond** and his swordsman **Rhen** are hired protection.
* `b_orvelle` — Orvelle Abbey courtyard. Kidnappers in Black-Lion colours.
  Guests: Garmond, Adria (+Alisse, Lavinia in the scene). Post: **Delan** — thought
  dead — rides off with the princess on a kwehbo. Rhen: "…Delan?! You're alive!?"
  Flash back "one year earlier".

### Chapter I — "The Low-Born" (9 battles)
Rhen & Delan are cadets of the **Galwyn** academy under the Northsky banner, hunting the **Ashen Brigade**.
1. `b_galwyn` — brigands raid Galwyn's market. Delan guest.
   Between: flashback at **Castle Ygress** — Baldric's deathbed: charge to Dorian and Zander, "never shame the name" to Rhen; Rhen promises to look after Alys; Delan and Tessa adopted into the household.
2. `b_mandrel` — Mandrel Plains: rescue **Argan Vire** from brigade squires (choice: "Save Argan!" vs "Rout the enemy"). Argan's Marquis **Elmond** was kidnapped by the brigade.
   Between: Ygress — Argan begs Dorian; Dorian refuses (Zander is away); cadets put on guard duty. Rhen & Delan decide to act.
3. `b_swiggle` — Swiggle Woods: wolves, goblins & a bomb on the road to Dorhaven.
4. `b_dorhaven` — Dorhaven streets: brigade archers & wizards.
5. `b_sandrat` — Sandrat Cellar: brigade holding the Marquis. Post: **Wolfram** executes **Gustin** for dishonouring the brigade by kidnapping for ransom, releases the Marquis. Then at Ygress: Duke **Laurent** praises Rhen; secret scene: Laurent & Dorian had *paid* Gustin to stage the kidnapping.
6. `b_thieveskeep` — Thieves' Keep: **Mirelle** commands; defeated, she escapes. Delan's doubts grow.
   Between: the brigade raids Ygress and kidnaps **Tessa** (they think she is a Valorne). Argan sneers she's "only a commoner" — Delan attacks him; Rhen dismisses Argan.
7. `b_lenara` — Lenara Plateau: Mirelle again, killed; she argues nobles vs commoners with Rhen.
   Between: campfire night — Delan and Rhen talk of the stars, of Tessa, of powerless boys.
8. `b_fovain` — Fovain Mill: **Wolfram** (White Knight, holy sword arts) duel; he retreats after taking heavy damage (event at 50% HP ends the battle).
9. `b_ziekhold` — Fort Ziekhold: Zander refuses to bargain; **Argan** shoots Tessa and the hostage-taker. Battle: Argan + Northsky knights. Post: Delan cradles Tessa; the fort is fired; explosion swallows Delan. Rhen renounces his name and walks away.

### Chapter II — "Pawn and Player" (11 battles)
Present day. Rhen, Adria and Garmond pursue Delan and the princess.
1. `b_dorhaven2` — Dorhaven again: a Sanctum knight bribes a thief to "mislay" the princess; street battle vs Black-Lion soldiers/thieves.
2. `b_arawen` — Arawen Woods: save **Bocco** the kwehbo (Wolfram's old mount) from goblins/panthers (Bocco guest; recruitable after).
3. `b_zirkel` — Zirkel Falls: Delan protects Oriane from Northsky assassins; **Garmond betrays** and fights you alongside the knights. Delan and Rhen's tense talk; Rhen takes Oriane toward the Church.
4. `b_zelland` — Zelland: save **Mattis** from Baird Company thugs (Mattis guest; joins after).
5. `b_barrowhill` — Barrow Hill: ambush on the road to Lyonesse. Cardinal Dracomir receives the party; Oriane & Adria stay at the castle.
6. `b_zigor` — Zigor Fen: undead and marsh beasts on the way to Cogsgard.
7. `b_cogsgard` — Cogsgard: Baird Co. hold Mattis; Rhen hands over a fake stone; battle. Mattis's father **Bastian**; the **Zodiac Stone of Taurus**.
   Between: Delan meets Rhen on the road — "Everyone is swept along by the current. I'm swimming against it."
8. `b_barrowvale` — Barrow Vale: Adria, "released" by the Cardinal's men as bait, is ambushed; rescue her.
9. `b_golgrand` — Golgrand Gallows: Garmond and knights ambush you.
10. `b_lyonesse` — Castle Lyonesse gate: final fight with **Garmond** (he dies).
11. `b_vepar` — Castle Lyonesse chapel: Cardinal Dracomir transforms into **Vepar the Defiled King** (Scorpio). Oriane is gone — Delan took her to Duke Galtran. The Pride War begins.

### Chapter III — "The Brave and the Damned" (11 battles)
1. `b_colgrave` — Colgrave: save **Oren Durant** from thieves (guest).

## 3. Content conventions (for everyone writing data)

* All content is **TypeScript data** in `src/data/**`. Each file exports arrays named
  `jobs`, `monsters`, `abilities`, `items`, `characters`, `maps`, `battles`, `scenes`,
  `story`, `side`, `nodes`, `edges`, `shopStock`, `errands`, `artefacts`, `chronicle`.
  `src/data/db.ts` auto-collects them. **Ids are camelCase and globally unique per kind.**
* Schemas: `src/data/types.ts` (read it fully). Formula helpers: `src/battle/formulas.ts`
  (`F.*`). Special handlers: `src/battle/specials.ts` (`special: 'jump' | 'throw' | 'iaido' |
  'geo' | 'geoAuto' | 'calc' | 'randomStrikes' | 'passive' | 'blink'`, and effect specials
  `'wish' | 'fullRestore' | 'gravity' | 'instantKo' | 'balance' | 'gilSteal'`).
* Reference examples: `src/data/jobs/squire.ts`, `src/data/jobs/chemist.ts`,
  `src/data/items/consumables.ts`, `src/data/maps/training.ts`.
* Charge time `ct` is in clockticks (source "CTR"): quick spells 2–4, big spells 6–10.
* Magic damage: `F.magic(q)` with source Q values (Fire 14, Fira 18, Firaga 24, Flare 46…).
  Status spells: `hit: F.hitMa(base)` + `effects: [{type:'status', add:[...]}]`.
* `vfx` must be a `VfxId` (see types). Add `color` for tint.
* **Never** use the source game's proper nouns (characters, places, factions, skill
  names that are trademarks). Generic fantasy words (Fire, Cure, Haste, Knight, Ninja,
  Potion, Phoenix Down) are fine.
* Don't run git. Only create/edit the files you're assigned. Run `npx tsc --noEmit` and
  `npx vitest run` before finishing and fix errors in *your* files.

### Map authoring
Cell token `<height><terrain>[flags][/slope]`, see `MapDef` docs in types.ts.
* Heights are in "h" (one h = one step a unit can jump with Jump 1). Typical maps range 0–8h, towers up to 12h.
* Make maps 10–16 wide, 10–16 deep. Every deploy cell and enemy cell must be standable
  and reachable. Use elevation, water, choke points, rooftops — the source's maps are
  famous for verticality. Towns: buildings are tall columns of `b`/`o` with `t` roof
  cells on top (units can stand on roofs). Forests: `T`/`P` tree flags (unstandable).
* `decor` adds props (see `DecorDef.type`). Put `chimney`, `banner`, `lamp`, `crate`,
  `barrel`, `fence`, `well`, `cart`, `market` stalls to make towns lived-in.
* `deploy` lists the player's start cells (at least 6).

### Scenes
Scenes are arrays of `SceneCmd` tuples. Actors are addressed by id (for named
characters use the character id). `['actor', id, charIdOrJobId, x, z, facing]` spawns.
Dialogue is `['say', id, text]` — keep each line ≤ 180 chars, split long speeches.
Use `['emote']`, `['anim']`, `['move']`, `['camera']` and `['wait']` to stage things.
Narration uses `['narrate', text]`.

## 4. Story authoring guide (maps, battles, scenes, story steps)

### Files & ordering
Each story agent writes its own files (never edit someone else's):
`src/data/maps/<part>.ts`, `src/data/battles/<part>.ts`, `src/data/scenes/<part>.ts`, `src/data/story/<order>_<part>.ts`.
STORY steps are concatenated in **path sort order**, so story files are named
`story/00_prologue_ch1.ts`, `story/10_ch2.ts`, `story/20_ch3.ts`, `story/30_ch4a.ts`, `story/31_ch4b.ts`.
Side quests export `side` (SideQuestStep[]) from `story/90_side.ts`.

### How the game runs a story step
1. The world map highlights `step.at` (red node). The player travels there (or, if `at` is omitted, the step
   runs immediately after the previous one — use for back-to-back events like prologue → Galwyn flashback).
2. `pre` scene plays (on its own `map`), then the `battle` (deploy screen → fight), then the `post` scene.
3. On completion: battle id is set as a flag (e.g. `b_galwyn`), plus `flags`, `unlock` nodes appear, `tier` raises
   shop stock, `join` characters enter the party, `moveTo` relocates the party marker.
   A step may be scene-only (no battle) — e.g. the Ygress conversations between battles.
4. Losing a battle → Game Over (retry from the deploy screen or load).

### Party level & enemy levels
`level: '+2'` means party level + 2 (party level = average of the 5 highest-level party members).
Use relative levels for rank-and-file; bosses may use `'+3'`..`'+5'` and `hpMult` 1.5–3 (Umbral lords 3–5).
Early Chapter I battles: party level ≈ 1–8; Ch.II ≈ 8–18; Ch.III ≈ 18–30; Ch.IV ≈ 30–45.

### Unit spawns
* Generic enemies: `{ job: 'knight', level: '+1', at: [x,z], facing: 'S', team: 1 }` — equipment and abilities are
  **auto-assigned** by the engine from the job + current shop tier if `equip`/`learned` are omitted. Add
  `secondary`, `reaction`, `support` to make them interesting. Give memorable enemies a `name`.
* Named characters: `{ char: 'wolfram', level: '+3', at, boss: true, hpMult: 2 }` (id defaults to the char id).
  Their job comes from CharacterDef unless you set `job` (e.g. Delan in Ch.II+ → `job: 'lionKnight'`).
* Guests (AI allies): `team: 0, ai: 'aggressive'` (or 'defensive'/'support'); add `vip: true` + list in `protect`
  if their fall loses the battle.
* Monsters: `{ job: 'goblin', level: '+0', at, team: 1 }`.
* `hidden: true` units appear later via a BattleEvent script `['reveal', id]` (reinforcements).
* Keep deploy cells free of spawns. Enemies start 5–10 tiles away from deploy cells.

### Victory conditions
`defeatAll` (default), `defeat` (boss ids), `defeatAny`, `survive` (N hero turns), `reach` (cells, e.g. the sluice lever).
A boss retreat mid-battle: `events: [{ when: { hpBelow: ['wolfram', 50] }, script: [['say','wolfram','…'], ['retreat','wolfram'], ['battleEnd','victory']] }]`.

### Scenes
* `map` = scene stage (can be a battle map or a dedicated small scene map like `scene_ygress_hall`; author scene maps
  in your maps file with `deploy` set to any 5 standable cells).
* Spawn actors first: `['actor', 'rhen', 'rhen', 4, 6, 'N']` (id, character-or-job id, x, z, facing).
  Generic extras: `['actor', 'guard1', 'knight', 2, 3, 'S', { name: 'Northsky Guard', team: 1 }]`.
* Refer to the hero by `{hero}` in text (the player may rename him); his family name is Valorne.
* Dialogue: `['say', id, 'text']`, ≤ 180 chars per line; use many short lines, like the source's dialogue boxes.
  Use `{ mood: 'shout' }` for shouts, `'think'` for inner thoughts, `'whisper'`.
* Stage directions: `['move', id, x, z]`, `['face', id, 'E' | otherId]`, `['anim', id, 'kneel']`,
  `['emote', id, '!']`, `['camera', { at: id, zoom: 0.8, time: 1.2 }]`, `['wait', 0.6]`, `['fade','out',1]`,
  `['music', 'somber']` (music ids: title prologue worldmap town tavern battle1 battle2 battle3 boss umbral
  finalBoss victory defeat somber tension church heroic romance campfire dungeon ending credits chapter formation),
  `['sfx', 'thunderclap']`, `['vfx', 'holy', 'actorId']`, `['shake', 0.3, 0.6]`, `['flash', '#ffffff', 0.8]`.
* Narration between scenes: `['narrate', 'text']`; chapter cards: `['title', 'Chapter I', 'The Low-Born']`.
* Chronicle unlocks: `['chronicle', 'ev_campfire']` (ids from src/data/world/chronicle.ts).
* Party changes: `['join', 'mattis']`, `['leave', 'adria']`. Items: `['item', 'elixir', 1]`, `['gil', 500]`.
* Choices: `['choice', 'What will you do?', [['Save Argan!', [...cmds]], ['Rout the enemy!', [...cmds]]]]` — use
  `['flag', 'name']` inside branches if later text depends on it, then `['if', 'name', thenCmds, elseCmds]`.
* A scene should feel directed: establish shots with camera moves, let characters walk in, pause with `wait`,
  use emotes. Fill in the drama the source only implies — the political intrigue, Delan's bitterness, Rhen's
  doubt, the Church's hypocrisy — in an earnest, slightly archaic register. Original prose only.
